# Continuous-bullying robustness model

R1 replaces the categorical bullying measure with ASBGSB. Higher ASBGSB values indicate less bullying, so a positive coefficient means that less bullying is associated with higher reading achievement.

Cells report coefficients with jackknife standard errors in parentheses. 
Significance: *** p < 0.01, ** p < 0.05, * p < 0.10.
